  • Day 4: Halong Bay - Hanoi - Flight to Da Nang: Morning Tai Chi session (optional). Breakfast onboard while cruising back. Disembark. Transfer back to Hanoi Airport for flight to Da Nang (DAD). Arrival in Da Nang, transfer to Hoi An. Check into hotel. Overnight in Hoi An.
    Focus: Scenic cruising, transition to Central Vietnam.
  • Day 5: Hoi An Ancient Town Discovery: Morning guided walking tour of Hoi An Ancient Town (UNESCO site). Visit Japanese Covered Bridge, ancient merchant houses, Phuc Kien Assembly Hall. Afternoon free for exploring boutiques, tailor shops, or relaxing by the beach (An Bang). Optional cooking class or countryside cycling tour. Overnight in Hoi An.
    Focus: Historical architecture, charming atmosphere, artisan crafts, coastal proximity.
  • Day 6: Hoi An - Da Nang - Flight to Ho Chi Minh City: Morning free or optional visit to My Son Sanctuary (Cham ruins). Transfer to Da Nang Airport (DAD) for flight to Ho Chi Minh City (SGN - Tan Son Nhat International Airport). Arrival, meet & greet, transfer to hotel. Check-in. Overnight in Ho Chi Minh City (HCMC).
    Focus: Transition to Southern Vietnam, introduction to the bustling metropolis.
  • Day 7: Ho Chi Minh City Tour & Cu Chi Tunnels: Morning excursion to the famous Cu Chi Tunnels, an immense network used during the Vietnam War. Afternoon city tour of HCMC: visit War Remnants Museum, Reunification Palace, Notre Dame Cathedral (exterior), Central Post Office. Explore Ben Thanh Market. Overnight in HCMC.
    Focus: Modern history, war legacy, colonial landmarks, vibrant city life.
  • Day 8: Mekong Delta Excursion (My Tho/Ben Tre): Full-day trip to the Mekong Delta. Boat trip on the Mekong River, visit local villages, fruit orchards, coconut candy workshop. Experience sampan ride through smaller canals. Lunch featuring local specialties. Return to HCMC in the late afternoon. Overnight in HCMC.
    Focus: Rural life, river culture, agricultural heartland, unique delta experiences.
  • Day 9: Ho Chi Minh City - Free Day / Optional Activities: Day at leisure for personal exploration, shopping, revisiting favorite spots, or optional tours (e.g., Cao Dai Temple, Fine Arts Museum). Farewell dinner possibility. Overnight in HCMC.
    Focus: Flexibility, deeper exploration based on personal interest, souvenir shopping.
  • Day 10: Departure from Ho Chi Minh City: Check out from hotel. Transfer to Tan Son Nhat International Airport (SGN) for onward flight back to India.
    Focus: Smooth departure coordination.
